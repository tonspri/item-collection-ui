# v1.4.0
- Stats tab: read a card's effects in the panel, and pick which tab opens first in the gear menu.
- Search by name, card effect or collection bonus.
- Types button: hide normal, boss or MVP cards, in searches too.
- New setting: store extra copies above a card's limit, with a To Limit button. Bulk Deposit follows it.
- Cards in your bag now glow and show how many you carry, with an In Bag filter.
- Combos show what you can complete from your bag, with one-click Deposit from Bag and a Withdraw Set button.
- Withdrawing asks first if it would drop a card below complete.
- With bonuses off, the Combos, Bonuses and Collect tabs are hidden.
- New combo, Night Terrors, and combos can now use bonus2 effects that simply add up.
- Collection data carries over.
