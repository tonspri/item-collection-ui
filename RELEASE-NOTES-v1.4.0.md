Find cards faster, see what you're carrying, and use the collection as your card storage.

- **See your bag:** cards you carry that the collection can take glow green and show how many. A new **In Bag** filter lists them.
- **Combos from your bag:** sets you can finish light up. **Deposit from Bag** adds what the set still needs in one click, and **Withdraw Set** takes the set's cards back after showing you a list.
- **Store extra copies:** deposit above a card's limit, up to 9,999, and keep your bonuses. It's off by default. Turn it on in **Settings → Mods → Item Collection UI → Let the collection store extra copies**, then **Apply & restart**. Adds a **To Limit** button, and Bulk Deposit takes every copy.
- **Stats tab:** read a card's effects in the panel, and choose which tab opens first in the gear menu.
- **Search by** name, card effect or collection bonus.
- **Types button:** hide normal, boss or MVP cards, in searches too.
- **New combo: Night Terrors.** Combos can now use `bonus2` effects that simply add up, and the README explains how to add your own.
- With bonuses off, the Combos, Bonuses and Collect tabs are hidden.
- Withdrawing asks first if it would drop a card below complete.
- Your collection and bonuses carry over.
