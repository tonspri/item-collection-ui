Collection goals with their own bonuses, and a chat line when you find a card you still need.

- **Milestones:** extra bonuses for completing 10%, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of the era's cards. Each goal is marked on the progress bar and listed under **Bonuses** with your progress.
  - **10%:** STR, AGI, VIT, INT, DEX and LUK +1
  - **25%:** Max HP and Max SP +2%
  - **50%:** all six stats +1, ATK and MATK +10
  - **75%:** Max HP and Max SP +3%
  - **100%:** all six stats +2, ATK and MATK +20
- Withdrawing below a goal pauses its bonus. Deposit again and it comes back. A Bulk Deposit that reaches a goal says so in its summary.
- **Needed card alerts:** when you pick up a card your collection still needs, autoloot included, a chat line shows how many you've deposited and how many you carry. Turn it off in the gear menu.
- Your collection and bonuses carry over.
