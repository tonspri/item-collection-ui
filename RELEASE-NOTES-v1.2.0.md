First public release of Item Collection UI.

- A card collection window shared by your whole account. Deposit cards from your inventory to complete them, and every character earns a small permanent bonus.
- Nine themed Combo Collections with an extra bonus. Combos use no cards up.
- Real card art from your own game files, with filters, slots and search.
- Open it from the card icon in the Basic Info menu, or type `@collection`.
- Works in renewal and pre-renewal. Fees, copies needed and bonuses can be changed in Settings.
